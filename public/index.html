NDI, OBS & Network Stage Outputs

Connect any device or broadcast switcher on your local network to Grace Flow.

Paste into an OBS/vMix Browser Source (1920x1080). Background is 100% transparent for camera overlays!

🎥 How to Stream Over NDI to the Same Network:

  1. Method 1 (OBS NDI / vMix NDI Output): Add the Transparent Browser Source link above into OBS or vMix, then turn on NDI Output in OBS/vMix. Any NDI receiver on the same network will receive the stage feed at zero latency.
  2. Method 2 (NewTek NDI Screen Capture): Click "Send to Second Screen" to open the projection window, open the free NewTek NDI Screen Capture app, and select the Grace Flow window as an NDI device.
  3. Method 3 (Direct Wi-Fi Device): No NDI hardware needed — any smartphone, iPad, or computer connected to the church Wi-Fi can open the Stage Monitor URL directly in Safari or Chrome.
