Frequently Asked Questions

Does Grace Flow need an internet connection?

No. Both the desktop apps (Mac/Windows) and the web app run 100% offline. Fonts, synthesizer chime audio, and timer engines are fully bundled locally so your service is never vulnerable to flaky church Wi-Fi.

How do I connect an iPad or stage monitor wirelessly?

Connect your computer and stage iPads to the same church Wi-Fi router (no internet required on the router). In Grace Flow's Settings deck, it displays your local IP address (e.g. http://192.168.1.50:3000/stage). Type that into Safari on the iPad!

Can I send lower-third countdowns to OBS Studio or vMix?

Yes! Add a Browser Source in OBS or vMix pointing to http://localhost:3000/stage?transparent=true. The timer and stage message float with complete alpha channel transparency over your live camera video.

Can I count down to an exact clock time?

Yes. Enter your target service start time (e.g. 7:30 AM), and Grace Flow will automatically calculate the countdown down to the exact second.

Is it really completely free?

Yes. Grace Flow is 100% free, open-source, and has no ads, no subscriptions, and no account requirements.
