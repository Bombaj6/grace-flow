Frequently asked questions

Does it need internet?

No. The app runs 100% offline once installed — fonts, themes, and everything else are bundled. This is deliberate: church tech should never depend on a flaky connection during service.

How does NDI® and OBS streaming work?

GraceFlow automatically broadcasts an NDI® service on your local network on port 8080/5353. On the same Wi-Fi, apps like OBS Studio (with NDI plugin), vMix, and ProPresenter will automatically see GraceFlow as a video input. You can also turn on "Transparent Background" mode in ⚙️ Settings to key countdown lower-thirds over your live camera video feeds!

Do I need a second screen?

No — you can run it on one laptop. But the app is built for the common setup: operator screen on the laptop, congregation display on a projector or TV via HDMI. One click moves the display to the second screen and makes it fullscreen.

Can I count down to the exact service start time?

Yes — that's the headline feature. Pick “Target Clock Time,” enter e.g. 7:30 AM, and the display counts down to the exact minute. If the time has already passed, it targets tomorrow. You can also run classic duration timers (10:00, 30:00…) for sermons and segments.

How do I show announcements?

The display splits into the giant countdown on the left and a rotating announcement deck on the right. Build your slides in the operator dashboard, toggle the deck on or off, or switch to a clean timer-only view at any moment.

Is it really free?

Yes. No license, no subscription, no ads. If it's useful to your church, tell another church about it.
